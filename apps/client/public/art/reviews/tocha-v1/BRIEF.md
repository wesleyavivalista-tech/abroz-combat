## Gameplay brief reserved for after appearance approval

- Basic 1: repeated-button punch sequence ending with a kick; hands and feet ignite during attacks.
- Basic 2: heavy fire kick.
- Basic 3: heavy double punch with intense hand fire; a hit ignites the opponent for 2 seconds of continuous damage.
- Special Fireball: projectile hit ignites opponent for 3 seconds of continuous damage. Every fighter needs a burning presentation; burning does not restrict movement.
- Special Heavy Metal pentagram: place a pentagram at Tocha's position for 4 seconds; nearby opponents take damage.
- Super On fire: Tocha fully ignites for 10 seconds. Touching enemies ignite for 3 seconds of continuous damage. Movement and attacks are 50% faster during the effect.
- Super 666 Satan: shirtless appearance revealing chest tattoo; rock hand sign and headbanging; large pentagram appears around head, then launches at an opponent for very high damage.
- Existing shared constraints: two full-meter, once-per-fight supers; team attacks must respect friendly-fire rules.

Exact damage values, burn stacking/refresh behavior, proximity radius, speed-modifier frame rounding, target selection and interruption behavior remain to be designed during implementation after approval.
