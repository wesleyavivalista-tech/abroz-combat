FINAL DEATH, GRAVES AND ZOMBIES — IMPLEMENTATION NOTES

Behavior
- Final-life KOs record their position and trigger an 18-simulation-tick
  cinematic. The shared client fixed-step clock runs at 30% speed during it
  (approximately one second). Gameplay, status deadlines, platforms, projectiles,
  fighter animations and effect updates all follow that slowed clock. Network
  input frames remain ordinary sequential frames; no nondeterministic gameplay
  timers or extra input messages were introduced.
- Camera focuses on the eliminated fighter; simultaneous deaths are framed
  together. The corpse remains visible through the short cinematic. Result UI
  waits for the sequence and the existing network confirmation requirement.
- DEAD uses local device speech at a lower pitch/rate, with the existing mute,
  volume, focus and pause safeguards. Voice availability/quality varies by device.
  Final-death speech waits for confirmed input frames online; rapid deaths do
  not repeatedly interrupt the word. The low impact sound and DEAD text also
  work without an installed local English voice. No proprietary recordings used.
- Standard two-seat matches do not create graves. Training has no final-life
  mechanic. Forfeits/disconnections do not create graves or fake killing blows.
- During continuing 3+ fighter matches, a 500-HP grave appears at the exact
  death coordinates. Airborne death coordinates are retained, not projected to
  the floor. Graves stay anchored (including when a moving platform moves away).
  They have progressive cracks and no health bar or companion health label.
- Destruction produces fragments and a one-second resurrection. The zombie
  reuses the original fighter/skin artwork with a sickly tint, slower gait,
  slouched pose, claw trails, rise/hit/collapse presentation. No independent
  per-character sprite sheets or duplicated character controllers were added.
- Zombies retain their source fighter's maximum HP, have one life and no further
  resurrection. Their AI targets living player fighters irrespective of teams.
  Zombie visuals use shared original attack poses adapted to the claw timeline.
- New NPC defaults: 2.2 ground speed / 3.8 air drift, 48-damage claw, 18-tick
  windup, 84-tick attack interval, 180-tick poison with 10 damage every 30 ticks.
  Incoming normal attacks, blocking, damage, poison, statuses and stage surfaces
  reuse existing systems. The original fighters' balance values were not changed.
- Navigation selects reachable landing areas, double-jumps, follows moving
  platforms, drops from higher surfaces and jumps when blocked by walls. It is
  intentionally simple rather than a full tactical pathfinder.
- Undead reuse combat companion storage/identity, without player input, player
  respawns, team affiliation or winner eligibility. Neutral target checks are
  shared across melee, projectiles, beams and area/status interactions. Round
  cleanup removes them; snapshot/rollback includes their complete AI state.

Validation
- Full TypeScript test suite, typecheck, lint, client production build, server
  build. Focused tests cover every fighter's HP/lifetime, melee/projectile
  contacts, all roster moves through shared hit resolution, former-teammate
  attacks, poison duration, interrupted claw timing/hitstop, simultaneous KOs,
  six simultaneous zombies in an eight-seat match, exact grave placement,
  standard 1v1, forfeits, round reset, all arenas' highest platforms, moving
  platforms, walls, target changes, delayed-input rollback/hash agreement,
  voice confirmation/deduplication and visual/material cleanup.
- Visual tests exercise Three.js scene objects and transforms with a test fighter
  visual. They do not substitute for inspecting the shipped art in a live match.
- Live browser visuals, listening to DEAD, two-device online play and GPU/frame
  rate performance were not verified in this session. Browser access was blocked
  earlier in this chat; no alternate browser-control path was used to bypass it.

ILLUSTRATED SPRITE REPLACEMENT — 2026-10-07
The procedural 3D ZombieModel was removed after art-direction review. All 16
fighters now have dedicated transparent, hand-illustrated zombie sprite sheets
in apps/client/public/art/zombies. Each sheet has eight poses: idle, three walk
poses, claw anticipation, contact, hurt and collapse. The existing sprite
isolation pipeline extracts silhouettes without neighbouring-frame bleed.
ZombieSpriteRenderer uses unlit transparent planes, like the fighter renderers.
Pixel scale is anchored to idle; feet are grounded; walk poses advance with
movement; claw contact follows the existing hit frame. Emergence reveals the
idle illustration from below the surface. Dedicated jump/fall illustrations
were added in the follow-up below. No additional simulation changes in this replacement.
Alternate living skins currently share that character's original-outfit zombie.

Assets were generated with the built-in image_gen tool, using the game's
Nikalz combat art for the initial style and each fighter's existing victory art
for identity. Exact prompts and source/output paths: public/art/zombies/prompts-v1.json.
All 16 sheets were visually inspected and decoded to check transparency and
8 isolated silhouettes each. 795 tests, full typecheck and lint passed.
Live browser animation/performance and two-device multiplayer were not verified.

DASH AND AIRBORNE ART FOLLOW-UP — 2026-10-07
Dodo's normal skins were explicitly bypassing DashVisual, showing upright walk
frames with a whole-body tilt. Both now use the existing dedicated forward
lunge/backward retreat sheet, with its per-skin standing scale anchor. Summon
dash artwork and simulation timing are unchanged.

All 16 zombies now select dedicated illustrated jump and fall frames. Four
shared air sheets contain three columns (idle scale anchor, jump, fall) and
four character rows. They are cached once per group, normalized to each row's
idle, and disposed with the zombie assets. Stun, claw and death retain priority.
Both match rendering and the lineup use this renderer; no simulation edits.
Built-in image_gen prompts and output provenance: public/art/zombies/prompts-air-v1.json.

Validated all 48 new silhouettes and Dodo's existing six with the production
sprite-isolation function. Added coverage for both Dodo skins/directions,
interruption, zombie jump/fall/landing, airborne scale and complete roster art.
796 tests, full typecheck, lint and production build passed.
Live browser animation and performance could not be verified.

GRAVE ART AND MOVEMENT FOLLOW-UP
Replaced the plain extruded grave with an illustrated weathered stone cross:
carved inset border, diamond medallion, chipped edges, moss and stepped base.
Shared cached transparent artwork fits within the existing grave hurtbox;
damage cracks and emergence debris are preserved. Asset and generation prompt:
public/art/zombies/grave-cross-v1.png and prompt-grave-v1.json (built-in image_gen).
Zombie pursuit ground/air horizontal movement is multiplied by 0.7. Attack
cadence, jump impulse, gravity and knockback are unchanged. Slower movement
exposed edge reversal while leaving a platform; exit selection now follows the
target's side and continues across the initial edge step. Navigation tests allow
longer travel and keep distractors away from the moving-platform test target.
797 tests, typecheck, lint and production build pass. Live visual playback was
not verified; art inspected directly and transparency confirmed.

ILLUSTRATED GRAVE DESTRUCTION
Generated grave-break-v1.png from the approved intact cross, with intact scale
reference, light damage, heavy damage, critical damage, separating chunks and
rubble. The original healthy artwork remains in use. Damage stages retain the
previous 22/48/72-percent damage thresholds; emergence displays critical damage
for 10 ticks, separating chunks through tick 23, then rubble fading over the
last 18 rise ticks. Removed procedural crack lines and 3D debris entirely.
Fixed-cell extraction retains disconnected stone fragments; all stages use the
same pixel scale and ground anchor, so rubble does not enlarge to tomb height.
No health, collision, movement, damage or resurrection timing changes.
Prompt/provenance: public/art/zombies/prompt-grave-break-v1.json (built-in image_gen).
44 relevant tests, full typecheck, lint and production build passed. Inspected
art and all six alpha bounds; live browser animation remains unverified.
