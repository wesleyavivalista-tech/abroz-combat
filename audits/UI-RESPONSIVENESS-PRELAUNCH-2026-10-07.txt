PRE-LAUNCH UI / RESOLUTION / FULLSCREEN / BROWSER AUDIT
2026-10-07

UI BUGS FOUND
1. Pause heading could be clipped above the scroll origin on short screens.
   Reproduction: open pause at 960x540; the first heading line was above the screen.
   Cause: flex centering within a height-limited scrolling drawer centers oversized
   content into negative space. Shrinking flex children compounded the problem.
   Fix: safe centering with a start-alignment fallback, and nonshrinking children.
   Existing roomy-screen centering, typography, colors and button design retained.
2. Eight-player pause lineup overflowed horizontally behind the command drawer.
   Reproduction: eight idle fighters, pause at 1280x720; players beyond P4 were off
   to the right and the full dialog acquired a horizontal scrollbar.
   Cause: unwrapped flex row sized for a two-player matchup.
   Fix: allow existing player entries to wrap. All eight now fit; no card redesign.
3. Canvas/postprocessing density remained at initialization/last quality setting
   after a resize accompanied by a device-pixel-ratio change.
   Cause: resize updated sizes and camera aspect but never updated pixel ratio.
   Fix: recompute density using the existing quality cap and update both renderer
   and composer only when it changes. No quality cap or lighting change.
   Confirmed by code inspection and regression tests. Physical monitor switching
   was not performed. A DPR-only change that does not trigger ResizeObserver still
   needs hardware validation; this fix runs on the existing resize path.

RESOLUTION-SPECIFIC PROBLEMS
Pause defects reproduced at 960x540 and 1280x720. Fixed pause measured at all seven
sizes below: dialog scrollWidth equaled clientWidth, scrollTop was zero and the
first content item was at positive y. Last Controllers action was successfully
reached through the scrolling drawer. Larger screens retain centered content.
Settings and controller lists intentionally scroll; controls below the viewport
are not classified as clipping when reachable in their scroll container.
Sixteen-player brackets intentionally scroll vertically and, where needed,
horizontally. Tested maximum-length unbroken participant names at 960x540.
