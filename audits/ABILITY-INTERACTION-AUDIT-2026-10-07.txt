SCOPE AND EVIDENCE
All 16 registered fighters and all 225 registered move definitions were included. Removed/unregistered prototype fighters are not part of the playable roster.
Reviewed move definitions, shared contact/damage/ownership rules, projectile pool reuse, each specialized ability system, lifecycle cleanup, arena/platform integration and deterministic disconnect/forfeit handling. No broad system rewrite.

NEW CONFIRMED BUG AND FIX
MEDIUM — Jopa football/chess duplicate energy credit.
Root cause: JopaSystem.strike called gainEnergy(source,3) and gainEnergy(owner,3). For football/chess, source IS owner, so one confirmed hit credited the same object twice (6 rather than one existing 3-point credit). Companion source and owner are distinct objects.
Reproduction: start Jopa at zero meter, make a football/chess projectile hit once. Before the fix meter becomes 6. Both dedicated regression tests failed with 6 != 3 before editing.
Fix: keep the source credit and perform the separate owner credit only when the references differ. The tentacle regression verifies its distinct owner still gets 3 and the companion still gets 3. Existing contact cooldowns prevent immediate re-credit.
Location: packages/game-core/simulation/JopaSystem.ts, strike().
Risk: low; limited to duplicate aliasing in energy attribution. This intentionally changes the accidental double credit, not an authored damage/cooldown/duration value.

COMMON TEST COVERAGE FOR EVERY FIGHTER
A. Each authored move against every roster target through the shared contact resolver: ally, dead and invulnerable targets must remain unchanged. 225 x 16 x 3 = 10,800 guard cases.
B. Each ability primed at its activation boundary, including early startup hazards, mode prerequisites, companion-only actions and Jomp reservations; two owners activate simultaneously. Branch through caster forfeit, target forfeit, ordinary caster death/respawn scheduling, match result, reset and snapshot replay. Check ownership cleanup, unique clone IDs and finite actor state.
C. Each ability in a four-seat simulation with a nearby ally and invulnerable opponent for 100 ticks. Check health, stun, traps, grabs, burn, poison and slow/loan markers. These bounded layouts verify protection policies, not every possible attack geometry.
D. Rerun existing per-fighter tests and previous all-roster state tests for startup interruption, recovery, mode expiry while attacking/stunned, death, simultaneous deaths, round transitions and repeated reset.
Fixture boundaries: activation fixtures bypass input cost/startup routing to reach uncommon states reliably. Those routes have separate existing tests. Forfeit tests exercise the deterministic gameplay path, not actual socket loss on physical machines.

CROSS-FIGHTER INTERACTION RISKS
- Strongest slow wins: new integration coverage combines Caio flood, Napa snot, Fausto cloud and Astolfo aura, verifies transition from 0.1 to 0.3 when the flood expires, and full restoration after leaving the areas. No multiplier changed.
- Independent damage clocks: two Tocha pentagrams each damage on cadence and cannot duplicate their own tick when revisited. Existing burn strength precedence and poison clocks remain unchanged.
- Shared ownership: an allied rider survives and regains movement when the knight owner forfeits. Summons, pooled projectiles and traps retain player ownership but use separate identity/hit stamps.
- Ambiguous priority left unchanged: loan locks versus mounts/grab armor; posthumous launched projectiles or already-applied statuses; differing house/solid interaction rules across beams, waves and projectiles. These are design-review questions, not newly confirmed defects.
- Capacity and array order are deterministic, but many players competing for global projectile/trap/summon limits can affect which new entity is admitted. No caps changed.
- No new critical crash or desynchronization was confirmed in this pass. Passing finite tests is not an exhaustive proof of every timing combination.
