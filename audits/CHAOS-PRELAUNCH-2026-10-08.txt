MEDIUM — FIXED: UNBOUNDED CONTROLLER IDENTITY HISTORY
Location: apps/client/src/input/GamepadManager.ts, discovery/update lifecycle.
Reproduction: join eight identical simulated Xbox pads, hold right + X, disconnect
one, reconnect it at a new browser index, reclaim its reserved seat, release all
inputs, clear between screens, repeat 128 times across all eight seats.
Before fix: 136 retained device records for eight current controllers.
Root cause: each reconnect creates a new session identity; obsolete disconnected,
unassigned records were never retired. The diagnostics panel and polling iterate
this growing map, so repeated reconnects increase memory and work indefinitely.
Fix: keep at most cap recent obsolete records. Always retain connected devices,
reserved player records, open test/identify records and queued event references.
After fix: <=16 records in the same stress case, with unique eight-seat ownership,
zero leaked held input and zero leaked join presses.
Regression risk: low. Very old unassigned disconnected entries disappear from
history; active/reserved identities and current diagnostics remain untouched.
