1v1 CHAMPIONSHIP — 30 September 2026

PLAY
Main menu > Local > Championship.
Local: choose 2–16 entrants, names, fighters and skins. Generate bracket, then
Play next match. The handoff names which entrant uses Player 1 / Player 2
controls. The confirmed winner advances and the bracket reopens after each duel.
Online: Main menu > Online, choose your fighter, then create a room with
mode 1v1 Championship and 2–16 players. Friends join using the five-letter code.
The bracket starts when the chosen number of players joins. Each assigned pair
confirms Ready for match. Other entrants remain on the bracket. Matches in a
round can run concurrently. The next round opens after every match finishes.

RULES
Single elimination, balanced seeded byes, fixed fighter/skin per entrant.
Registration order is seed order. Normal three-life 1v1 rules are preserved.
Draws replay the same pairing. Winners, eliminations, live/upcoming matches,
byes, forfeits and the champion are shown in the bracket.
Online disconnects withdraw that entrant and award their opponent a forfeit.
Unready entrants have two minutes to confirm. Stalled active players have the
existing loading/input grace periods (120 / 60 seconds). Waiting spectators
are not subject to gameplay inactivity timeouts. Refreshing/leaving forfeits;
rejoining an already started championship is not supported.

IMPLEMENTATION
packages/game-core/tournament/Championship.ts:
Shared bracket state and transitions, 2–16 entrants, round barrier and byes.
apps/server/src/championship.ts:
Independent server-confirmed simulation per duel. Results come from simulation,
not player-reported winners. Match-attempt IDs isolate inputs/hashes across
rounds and draw replays. Queues are bounded by the existing 120-frame lead limit.
apps/server/src/rooms.ts + index.ts:
Room integration, 16-player championship capacity, readiness, routing, forfeits.
packages/shared/protocol.ts:
Protocol v40, championship messages and match-scoped input/hash messages.
apps/client/src/ui/Championship.ts + championship.css:
Setup, bracket, local handoff, online readiness and champion screens.
apps/client/src/main.ts + ui/MenuScreens.ts:
Entry points, fresh simulation per duel, confirmed local results and online
server bracket updates. Existing FFA series and other modes remain available.
