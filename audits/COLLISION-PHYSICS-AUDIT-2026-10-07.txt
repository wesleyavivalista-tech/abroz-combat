PRE-LAUNCH COLLISION / PHYSICS AUDIT — 2026-10-07

PHYSICS BUGS FOUND
Confirmed: generic projectile blocking and fighter collision disagreed about target motion during a tick. StructureSystem compared a projectile sweep with the target's final box; GameSimulation used relative motion from the target's previous position. A wall could receive damage instead of a fighter crossed earlier. Conversely, a fighter arriving late at a point already crossed by the projectile could suppress a valid wall hit.
Reproduction: enemy-owned wall at x=500; shot moves from x=0 to 1000 at y=80. Enemy moves from x=200 to 800. Old blocking logic sees the wall first; relative motion correctly sees the fighter first. Second regression moves a target vertically from y=1000 to zero at x=200: final-position overlap incorrectly prevented the wall hit. These deliberately exaggerated one-tick trajectories isolate tunneling/contact-order behavior; they are not new gameplay speeds.
Root cause: two different time models for the same collision decision.
Severity: HIGH for incorrect combat collision; not a demonstrated crash or permanent lock.

HITBOX/HURTBOX BUGS
The confirmed issue above concerns swept hurtbox contact ordering. No authored attack box, size, damage, active window or recovery was changed.
Added checks cover startup and end-of-active-frame boundaries for every registered move, plus finite arena-bound knockback and high-speed floor landing for every registered character on all four arenas.
Transformation caution: hurtboxes and structure body collision account for weapon bodyScale, while fighter push separation scales cookieBoss only. This may intentionally preserve close combat spacing for large forms. Left unchanged because enlarging pushboxes would change reach and spacing without a confirmed design requirement. Visual review required for Eide mutant and Drupa White Buda.

FIXES IMPLEMENTED
StructureSystem.hitStructureProjectile accepts an optional read-only previous-position map. Its normal-projectile fighter-priority check now uses the same relative-motion sweep as GameSimulation. GameSimulation passes its existing map; no extra per-frame map or entity allocation was added.
Existing callers without movement history retain stationary-target behavior. Returning projectiles retain their existing separate path/blocking behavior. Equal-time structure contacts retain their existing priority.
Generated simulation revision updated so incompatible deterministic builds do not silently join.
Regression risk: low and confined to normal projectile-versus-structure ordering when targets move during the tick. No physics constants, assets, balance values, input or protocol fields changed.
