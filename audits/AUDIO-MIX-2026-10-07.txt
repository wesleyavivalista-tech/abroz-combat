SOURCE COMPENSATION
Once per decoded buffer, measure active 20ms RMS windows and peak. Target RMS .18 with correction bounded to .65–1.8, plus a .88 source peak constraint (which can reduce gain below .65). Silence cannot cause excessive boosts. Cache corrections alongside decoded buffers; evict together. Voice clips preserve their authored dynamics without correction. No source audio is destructively normalized.
