# Pixel fighter artwork

Generated with the built-in image_gen tool using HRvZaaxXsAAssrF.jpeg as a style reference. Original Vector and Brut designs; 16 transparent poses per fighter. Full atlases are preserved; renderer uses individual UV rectangles and foot pivots. Combat uses authored poses with tick-based anticipation, active and recovery stages, rather than a fully in-betweened frame-by-frame animation.

## prototype-a

Asset: apps/client/public/art/prototype-a-pixel-v1.png

Exact prompt:

Use case: stylized-concept. Create an original production sprite atlas for a 2D fighting game. The supplied image is a STYLE REFERENCE only: closely match its chunky detailed hand-pixelled arcade character style, bold dark 2-3 pixel outline, warm 4-tone skin ramps, intricate cloth folds, strong readable silhouettes, slightly enlarged head and fists, classic 1990s beat-em-up sprite craftsmanship. Do not copy the reference character's clothes or face. This must be true crisp pixel art with visible square pixel clusters, not smooth cartoon or 3D.
CRITICAL FORMAT: one SQUARE image, exactly FOUR columns by FOUR rows, sixteen equally-sized square cells with NO visible grid. One full-body fighter per cell, all facing RIGHT. Genuinely TRANSPARENT alpha background throughout, including gaps between limbs. NO checkerboard, background, floor, shadows, labels, letters or effects. Each sprite stays entirely inside its cell with generous empty padding. Consistent fighter proportions and pixel scale in all 16 poses; standing height about 75% of a cell, same ground baseline at 90% cell height; center hips horizontally at cell center. Wide kicks/punches stay within cell boundaries. Keep feet visible. No overlapping cells.
POSE ORDER left to right:
Row 1: relaxed boxing idle guard; second idle guard slightly bobbed; walking forward left foot forward; walking forward right foot forward.
Row 2: low crouching guard; standing block with both forearms protecting face; quick straight jab fully extended right; powerful long straight cross fully extended right with torso twist.
Row 3: standing side kick extended right; airborne tucked jumping pose; airborne flying side kick right; rising uppercut with front fist overhead.
Row 4: two hands thrust right in a special attack pose, no magic effects; leaning back recoiling from a hit; lying knocked down horizontally with head to LEFT and feet to RIGHT, entire body in cell; victory fist raised overhead.

CHARACTER: VECTOR: adult lean athletic tan-skinned male, short tousled dark hair, clean shaven expressive face, olive green zip-up track jacket with twin cream sleeve stripes and ribbed cuffs, charcoal jogger pants, off-white cloth hand wraps and white sneakers. Matches a modern street martial artist.
