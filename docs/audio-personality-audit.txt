PHASE LIMITS / AUTHORING NOTES
Normal short punches do not need an active loop or end sound: those slots are reserved and
not automatically played just because a short move exists. Channel loops start after startup.
Active modes, objects and sustained effects receive presence transitions. Hazards with the
same owner/move/kind/birth are coalesced for audio to reduce clutter.
Generic phase assets should describe only that phase; do not put launch and impact in the
same file. Impact is emitted by damage/contact, not on a miss. Composite recording layers
may be shared across compatible materials. Use 3–5 compatible impact variants.
Footsteps/running, distinct wall-body collisions, and mount hoof cadence currently have no
separate confirmed contact/step events; their identity is documented, but these detailed
foley triggers remain future work. There is no invented footstep clock.
Armor expiry is currently the armor-end hook; it does not prove armor was physically broken.
Structure collapse uses the move's end slot; use structural debris, not a generic explosion.
No subjective final-mix approval is claimed for audio that has no recordings.
